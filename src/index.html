While capturing

Tools

By hand: select the scrolling part, let go, then scroll with your mouse wheel from the top down. Snipzi records what you scroll past; press Enter or click Done when finished. Automatic: Snipzi turns the wheel itself, so keep the mouse still. Either way, select only the part that scrolls, without fixed side bars.
